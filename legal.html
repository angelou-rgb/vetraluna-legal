Gem Bench

Gem Bench is a reference and estimation tool, not a gemological report or identification.

  • Carat weights are estimates from measurements and standard shape factors. Actual weight can differ by 10% or more, especially for mounted, unusually proportioned or native-cut stones. Weigh stones on a calibrated scale for memos, invoices and sales.
  • Identification results are suggested matches ranked from the readings you enter. They are only as reliable as those readings, and they cannot separate natural from synthetic, detect treatments, or rule out composites and assembled stones.
  • Reference properties are typical published ranges. Individual stones can fall outside them.
  • Confirm identity, origin and treatment with standard gemological testing or an accredited laboratory before representing a stone to anyone.
  • Weight estimates are not offered for organic materials (pearl, coral, amber, jet, ivory, shell, ammolite), whose density and shape vary too widely for a reliable formula.
